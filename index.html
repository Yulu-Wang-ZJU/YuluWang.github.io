Yulu Wang 王雨璐

PhD student & machine learning researcher

I am a PhD student in Electronic Information at Zhejiang University, pursuing a master's-to-PhD track since 2024. My research develops reliable learning methods for long-horizon prediction, LLM reasoning, and multimodal data.

I study how models behave when observations are incomplete, conditions change, or prediction errors accumulate. My work spans stability analysis, uncertainty estimation, and clinical validation, building on an earlier background in FPGA and embedded systems.

An academic visit to Weill Cornell Medicine, Cornell University, is planned for 2027.
